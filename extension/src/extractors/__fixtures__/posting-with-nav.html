Software Engineer

Acme is looking for a Software Engineer to join our growing platform team. You will work on systems that process millions of events a day, alongside a small group of engineers who care about correctness as much as speed.

Requirements

  • 5+ years of professional experience with TypeScript or a similarly typed language
  • Experience designing and operating distributed systems in production
  • Comfort working across the stack, from storage to the API layer
  • Strong written communication, since most of the team works asynchronously

Responsibilities

You will design, build and maintain core platform services, partnering closely with product and design to ship complete features rather than isolated components. You will also mentor more junior engineers and participate in on-call rotations.
