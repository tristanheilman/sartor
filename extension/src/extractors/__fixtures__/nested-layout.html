Staff Platform Engineer

Initech is hiring a Staff Platform Engineer to lead the next generation of our internal deployment tooling. You will set technical direction for a team of eight and work directly with the VP of Engineering on the roadmap for the coming year.

What you will do

  • Own the architecture of the internal deployment and rollout system
  • Partner with security and compliance on access control for the platform
  • Review designs across multiple teams and unblock technical decisions
  • Hire and mentor senior engineers joining the platform group

What we are looking for

Eight or more years of experience building infrastructure at scale, a track record of technical leadership without direct reports, and a willingness to write code as well as documents.
