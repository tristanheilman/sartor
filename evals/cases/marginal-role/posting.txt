Senior Mobile Engineer (React Native) — Waypoint Mobility

Waypoint builds the driver-facing app for regional delivery fleets. About 60,000
drivers open it every morning and it has to work on a five-year-old Android
phone with two bars of signal. The mobile team is six people and owns the app
end to end: the React Native codebase, the native modules underneath it, and
the release pipeline that ships both stores every week.

What you will own
- The reliability of background location tracking. This is the heart of the
  product and the hardest thing we do — battery, OS restrictions, and accuracy
  all pull against each other.
- Release engineering. We ship weekly to both stores and want that to be dull.
- Payments. Drivers get paid through the app and we are mid-migration to a new
  processor.
- Mentoring two junior engineers who joined this year.

What we need
- Strong production experience with React Native and TypeScript. Required —
  this is the whole codebase.
- Experience with native iOS development in Swift or Objective-C. You will be
  writing bridge modules, not just JavaScript.
- Proficiency with CI/CD for mobile — Fastlane, GitHub Actions, or similar.
  Required. Weekly releases do not happen by hand.
- Experience integrating a payment processor such as Stripe.
- Familiarity with Firebase, particularly Cloud Messaging and Firestore.
- Track record of shipping to a large installed user base.

Nice to have
- Detox or Maestro for end-to-end mobile testing.
- GraphQL experience — we are moving that way next year.
- Kotlin for the Android side of bridge modules.
