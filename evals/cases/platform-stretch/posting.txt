Senior Platform Engineer — Meridian Logistics

We run the freight-tracking backbone for a few thousand carriers. The platform
team owns the services underneath: the event pipeline, the deploy story, and
the database layer everything else sits on.

What you will do
- Own the reliability of our event ingestion pipeline end to end, from producer
  to warehouse.
- Drive down p99 latency on the core write path. We have a number we care about
  and we are not hitting it.
- Take our deploys from "someone runs a script" to something the whole
  engineering org can use safely.
- Partner with product teams on schema design before they ship, not after.

What we need
- Strong experience with Go in production. This is most of what we write.
- Deep PostgreSQL knowledge — required. Sharding, replication, query planning,
  the parts that hurt at scale.
- Experience with Kafka or a comparable event streaming system.
- Proficiency with Kubernetes. Our whole runtime is on it and you will be
  expected to debug it under pressure.
- Experience with Terraform. All of our infrastructure is defined in it.
- Track record of mentoring engineers. Required — this is a senior role and the
  team is growing.

Nice to have
- Payments or logistics domain background.
- Experience with Datadog for observability.
