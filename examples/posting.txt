Senior Product Engineer — Lumen Fieldwork

Lumen Fieldwork makes the data-collection tools that environmental scientists
carry into the field: a web app for designing surveys, and the services that
sync, validate and publish what comes back. The product team owns the whole
slice, from the form a scientist fills in to the API that stores the answer.

What you will do
- Own features end to end: the React interface, the Node.js service behind it,
  and the PostgreSQL schema underneath.
- Keep the survey designer accessible. Our users include field staff who work
  with screen readers, and WCAG 2.1 AA is a release requirement, not a goal.
- Grow the shared design system that three product teams build on.
- Make the test suite something people trust: fast end-to-end coverage of the
  flows that matter, in Playwright or Cypress.

What we need
- Strong experience with TypeScript and React in production.
- Experience building and running Node.js services on PostgreSQL.
- A track record of shipping accessible interfaces.
- Experience with GraphQL. Our public API is a GraphQL schema and you will be
  evolving it.
- Proficiency with Kubernetes. Every service we run is deployed on it.

Nice to have
- Published open-source libraries, especially in the React ecosystem.
- Comfort working from Figma designs alongside a designer.
- Experience with Rust for the sync engine.
